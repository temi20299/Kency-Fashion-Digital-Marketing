A basic digital marketing strategy · 2026

Kency Fashion

A practical 30-day digital marketing plan designed to increase brand awareness, build trust, grow an engaged audience and generate enquiries and sales for a fashion business.

Prepared as a learning project
OverviewObjectivesAudienceStrategy30-day calendarMeasurement

1. Business overview

Kency Fashion is a fashion brand that promotes stylish clothing and outfit inspiration. The plan focuses on showcasing products clearly, helping potential customers make buying decisions and making it easy to contact the business.

Business

Kency Fashion

Industry

Fashion and clothing retail

Primary channels

Instagram, TikTok and WhatsApp Business

2. SMART marketing objectives

30 days

Brand awareness

Publish consistently for 30 days and aim to increase profile reach by 20% compared with the previous 30-day period.

15%

Engagement

Aim to improve interactions (likes, comments, shares and saves) by 15% over the previous month.

20

Enquiries

Generate at least 20 genuine product enquiries through direct messages or WhatsApp during the campaign.

These are proposed targets for the exercise. Actual performance should be measured against the business's starting figures.

3. Target audience

Demographics

Young adults and adults, approximately 18–35, especially people interested in everyday, smart-casual and occasion wear.

Location

Primarily customers in Nigeria, with emphasis on the areas where the business can deliver reliably.

Needs and interests

Affordable style, outfit ideas, clear sizing, quality photos, reliable customer service and convenient ordering.

4. Digital marketing strategy

Content marketing

Post product photos, styling tips, short try-on videos, customer feedback, behind-the-scenes clips and new-arrival announcements.

Social media

Use Instagram for product discovery and stories, TikTok for short entertaining videos, and WhatsApp Business for catalogues and customer conversations.

Customer conversion

Include a clear call to action in posts: send a DM, tap the WhatsApp link, ask about available sizes or place an order.

Customer relationship

Reply promptly, provide accurate prices and size information, request permission before sharing testimonials, and follow up politely with interested customers.

Suggested weekly posting rhythm

  • 3 short-form videos (TikTok and Instagram Reels).
  • 2 product or outfit photo/carousel posts.
  • Stories on most days for polls, stock updates and behind-the-scenes content.
  • 1 customer-focused post, such as a review, FAQ or styling tip.

Budget approach

Start with organic content and a small test budget only if funds are available. If running paid ads, test one product-focused campaign, define the audience and location, set a spending limit, and compare cost per enquiry before increasing spend. No fixed amount is assumed because the business budget has not been provided.

5. 30-day content calendar

A suggested schedule. Adjust product names, prices, availability and posting dates to match the real business.

6. Monitoring and evaluation

Awareness

Reach, impressions, video views and profile visits.

Engagement

Likes, comments, shares, saves, story replies and engagement rate.

Conversion

DMs, WhatsApp enquiries, orders and conversion rate.

Review the results every week. Identify the posts that attract the most saves, shares and enquiries. Repeat successful content formats, improve weaker posts and update the plan based on customer questions and sales results.

7. Conclusion

This basic strategy gives Kency Fashion a structured way to reach potential customers, communicate the value of its products and turn social media attention into enquiries and sales. Consistent content, clear calls to action, helpful customer service and regular measurement are essential to improving results.
