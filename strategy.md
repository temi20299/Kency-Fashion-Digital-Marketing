# Kency Fashion: Basic Digital Marketing Strategy

## 1. Business overview
Kency Fashion is a fashion and clothing brand. Its digital marketing should present products clearly, inspire customers with outfit ideas, build trust and make enquiries and ordering straightforward.

## 2. SMART objectives
For the first 30-day campaign, the proposed objectives are:
- Increase social-media reach by 20% compared with the previous 30-day period.
- Improve interactions (likes, comments, shares and saves) by 15%.
- Generate at least 20 genuine product enquiries through Instagram DMs or WhatsApp.

These are planning targets, not measured results. They should be reviewed against actual baseline figures.

## 3. Target audience
Young adults and adults, approximately 18–35, interested in everyday, smart-casual and occasion wear. The primary market is Nigeria, especially locations the business can serve reliably. Their interests include affordable style, outfit inspiration, clear sizing, quality product images and convenient ordering.

## 4. Channels and tactics
- Instagram: product photos, carousels, Stories, Reels, polls and customer FAQs.
- TikTok: short try-on clips, styling tips, transitions and behind-the-scenes videos.
- WhatsApp Business: catalogue, status updates, customer enquiries and order support.

Use clear calls to action such as “DM for available sizes,” “Message us to order,” and “Save this styling idea.” Reply promptly and give accurate product, price, size and delivery information.

## 5. Content approach
Use a mix of product promotion, educational styling tips, entertaining short videos, behind-the-scenes content, FAQs and customer feedback shared only with permission. A suggested weekly rhythm is three short videos, two product/photo or carousel posts, Stories on most days and one customer-focused post.

## 6. Budget
Begin with organic content. If the business has an advertising budget, test a small product-focused campaign with a defined audience, location and spending limit. Compare cost per enquiry before increasing spend. No fixed budget is assumed because the business's available funds are unknown.

## 7. Measurement
Review weekly:
- Awareness: reach, impressions, video views and profile visits.
- Engagement: likes, comments, shares, saves and story replies.
- Conversion: direct messages, WhatsApp enquiries, orders and conversion rate.

Repeat formats that lead to meaningful engagement and enquiries. Improve content that performs poorly and update the plan using customer feedback and results.

## 8. Conclusion
A consistent content plan, clear calls to action, helpful customer service and regular measurement can help Kency Fashion build awareness, trust and sales opportunities.
